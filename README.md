#Dashboard Development Steps

Data Aggregation & KPI Creation: Configured top-level KPI cards with custom icons to display core business metrics, including total Cuisines , Average Rating , Number of Restaurants, City count, Total Votes , and Price Range sum.

Slicers & Filter Controls: Integrated interactive slicing options along the top and left side panels to allow real-time filtering by Country Name (e.g., Australia, Brazil, India), Quarter (Qtr 1–4), Year Range (2010–2018 slider), and Month.

Categorical Bar Charts: Built horizontal bar charts to rank key metrics, specifically visualizing the Top 5 Cuisines by City, Top 5 Cities with Lowest Cuisines, and the Top 10 Restaurants & their No. of Cuisines (led by Cafe Coffee Day and Domino's Pizza).

Line Chart Trend Visualization: Incorporated a line chart titled Top 5 Restaurants with lowest Votes to track bottom-performing establishments across low vote metrics.


Proportional Donut Charts: Designed two donut charts to display proportion metrics:
Table Booking: Shows 87.88% "No" vs. 12.12% "Yes".
Online Delivery: Displays 74.34% "No" vs. 25.66% "Yes".
Column Chart Analysis: Created a vertical bar chart to highlight the Top 3 Cities with their Price Range

UI/UX Styling: Applied a high-contrast dark red and black theme, custom typography, shadow effects on visual cards, and relevant icons for better visual categorization.

Key Analytical Insights

1)Low Online Delivery & Booking Adoption: Only 25.66% of restaurants offer online delivery and 12.12% support table bookings in this dataset, indicating major opportunities for digital feature expansion.

2)Geographic Concentration: New Delhi dominates the price range volume , followed by Gurgaon) and Noida, showing heavy market concentration in the Delhi-NCR region.

3)Top Chains by Cuisine Variety: Cafe Coffee Day , Domino's Pizza, and Subway lead in offering the highest number of distinct cuisine options across locations.

4)Dominant Popular Cuisines: Chinese and North Indian  represent the most widely available cuisines across cities.
